Contact

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems: muutuup@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the game version (shown in Settings).

Common questions

A purchase isn't showing. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

How do I continue a run? Runs save automatically. Open the game and choose Continue.

I want the tips again. Settings › Replay Tips.

Is an internet connection needed? No. The game plays offline; it only goes online to buy or restore purchases.
